Contact

Email nhat@nhattranbuilds.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working. Replies usually within two working days.

Common questions

  • Restoring the one-time purchase on a new phone: open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Moving your log to a new phone: Settings → Backup creates a file you can save or send to yourself; open it on the new phone with Restore. Your log is not stored on any server, so there is no account to sign in to.
  • Reminders not arriving: check that notifications are allowed for the app in iOS Settings, and open the app at least once a week so upcoming reminders are refreshed.
  • Dose amounts: the app does not calculate or suggest doses. Every amount is one you enter, as instructed by your prescriber or the product label. Talk to your doctor about your treatment.
