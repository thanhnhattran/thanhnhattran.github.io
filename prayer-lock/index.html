Narrow Way — Support

An app for iPhone that locks the apps you choose until you pray or read today's Scripture, by Solutio Lab.

Contact

Email nhat@nhattranbuilds.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working. Replies usually within two working days.

Common questions

  • I need a locked app right now: open Narrow Way, go to Settings and tap Turn off all locks. All your apps open immediately. This is always free and you never need a subscription to do it.
  • Before you delete the app: tap Turn off all locks first, so nothing stays locked. Deleting the app also deletes your prayer journal.
  • Tapping Pray to unlock does nothing: the button sends a notification that brings you back to the app, so notifications must be allowed for Narrow Way in iOS Settings. If they are off, open Narrow Way yourself to pray.
  • Restoring a subscription on a new phone: open the paywall and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Moving your prayer journal: the journal is stored only on your iPhone and is not on any server. There is no account to sign in to, so it cannot be restored from us.
  • Cancelling a subscription or a free trial: subscriptions are handled by Apple. Follow Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.
