Narrow Way — Privacy Policy

Solutio Lab

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Your prayers and your app choices stay on your iPhone. Narrow Way has no account, no sign-in, and no server that stores what you write or which apps you lock.

Screen Time

Narrow Way uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps you choose. You pick them in a sheet provided by iOS, and your selection and your lock schedule are stored on your device. No Screen Time data leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising or advertising measurement.

Your prayers and journal

Prayers you type, including unfinished drafts, are saved on your device in your journal. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot read them. You can delete one prayer or the whole journal inside the app. Your device's own iCloud or computer backup may include the app's data, under Apple's terms; the app itself does not store your journal in iCloud.

Notifications

The "Time to pray" notification and the reminder before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your device by its operating system. No notification content leaves your phone.

Data we do receive

Usage analytics (PostHog). To learn which parts of the app work, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog — for example "a prayer was completed" and "the paywall was shown" — together with an anonymous random identifier created on install and basic technical details such as iOS version, device type, screen size and app version. When you choose apps, the only thing sent is how many apps, categories and websites you picked. These events never contain the names of your apps, your schedule, or the text of your prayers. They are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat). Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a subscription is valid and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous app user identifier. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What Narrow Way does not do

  • No advertising inside the app and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your prayers or your Screen Time data.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Deleting your data

Deleting a prayer in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device, including your journal. Anonymous analytics events are kept by PostHog for a limited period and cannot be tied back to you; if you want them removed, write to the address below with the date you installed the app.

Children

Narrow Way is for adults managing their own device. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change.
