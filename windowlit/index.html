Windowlit — Support

Project your phone onto a wall, window or house with a projector. By Solutio Lab.

Contact

Email nhat@nhattranbuilds.com. Please include your phone model, its operating system version and the projector you use if something is not working. Replies usually within two working days.

Common questions

  • The projector shows nothing or a black picture: check that the cable or adapter carries video (a charge-only cable does not), that the projector is on the right HDMI input, and that your phone supports video output. On some Android phones the connection only works with a USB-C port that supports video output. Open the connection screen in the app for the steps in order.
  • Samsung phones show a desktop instead of the app: turn off Samsung DeX, then reconnect the cable.
  • The picture disappears when the phone screen turns off: do not press the power button while a show is running. It turns off the picture too.
  • Restoring a purchase on a new phone: open the purchase screen or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Moving your projects to a new phone: projects are stored on the phone, not on a server, so there is no account to sign in to.
