Windowlit — Privacy Policy

Solutio Lab

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Your projects, videos and photos stay on your phone. Windowlit has no account, no sign-in, and no server that stores what you project or the videos and photos you choose.

Your projects

When you save a project, the names you give it, the position of the corners you dragged and the scenes or media you picked for each window or wall are stored on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete a project inside the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud, Google or a computer backup) may include the app's data, under Apple's or Google's terms; the app itself does not upload your projects anywhere.

Your own videos and photos

When you choose "your own video" or "your own photo", you pick the file in the picker built into iOS or Android. Windowlit does not ask for permission to your photo library, and it only receives the file you selected. The file is played on your phone and sent to your projector through the cable or wireless connection you set up. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

The projector connection

Sending the picture to a projector over a cable, AirPlay or a cast connection is handled by your phone's operating system. Windowlit does not read, record or store the name of your projector or your network.

Data we do receive

Usage analytics (PostHog). To learn which parts of the app work, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog. Examples: a projector was connected (and whether by cable, AirPlay, cast or screen mirroring), a project was fitted to a window, a built-in scene or "own video" or "own photo" was chosen, a show started or stopped (with a rough length such as "2 to 15 minutes" and the reason it stopped), the purchase screen was shown, a purchase or a restore completed, and the app was opened again after a break of two weeks or more. Each event comes with an anonymous random identifier created when you install the app and basic technical details such as the operating system version, device type and app version.

These events never contain your project names, the names or content of your files, the positions of your corners, or the name of your projector. They are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat). Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous app user identifier. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What Windowlit does not do

  • No advertising inside the app and no advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID).
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your projects, videos or photos.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. Anonymous analytics events are kept by PostHog for a limited period and cannot be tied back to you; if you want them removed, write to the address below with the date you installed the app.

Children

Windowlit is for adults setting up decorations at home. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change.
