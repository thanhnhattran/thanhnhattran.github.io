Contact

Email nhat@nhattranbuilds.com. Please include your phone model and its operating system version if something is not working. Replies usually within two working days.

Common questions

  • The reminder arrives late on Android: open Settings, then Reminders in the app, and tap "Allow exact timing". Without it, Android may deliver the reminder up to an hour late. Some phones also delay apps that use battery saving; allowing Tameby to run in the background helps.
  • No reminder at all: check that notifications for Tameby are allowed in your phone's settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in the app.
  • I missed a day: nothing piles up. Each day shows only that day's list.
  • Restoring a subscription on a new phone: open the purchase screen or Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Moving my edited schedule to a new phone: the schedule is stored on the phone, not on a server, so there is no account to sign in to. Edits made on one phone stay on that phone.
  • Cancelling: manage or cancel the subscription in the subscription settings of your Apple ID or Google account. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
