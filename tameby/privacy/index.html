Tameby — Privacy Policy

Solutio Lab

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Your cleaning schedule stays on your phone. This policy covers Tameby: Home Cleaning Routines on iPhone and Android. Tameby has no account, no sign-in, and no server that stores your tasks.

Your schedule and checkmarks

The daily time you choose, the tasks in your week (including tasks you add or rename), the tasks you tick off, your reminder settings and the last day you opened the app are stored on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party. Your phone's own backup (iCloud, Google or a computer backup) may include the app's data, under Apple's or Google's terms; the app itself does not upload your schedule anywhere. Because the schedule lives on each phone, changes you make on one device do not appear on another.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your phone for permission to show notifications. On Android it may also ask to allow exact timing ("Alarms and reminders") so the reminder arrives at the minute you chose. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no reminder content passes through our servers or a push notification service.

Data we do receive

Usage analytics (PostHog). To learn which parts of the app help people keep going, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog. Examples: the app was opened for the first time, a daily time was chosen, today's list was shown (with the weekday), a built-in task was ticked off, a day was edited (only the kind of change, such as "added" or "moved"), the reminder was set (with the hour and minute), the purchase screen was shown, a trial, purchase or restore completed, and the app was opened again after a gap of two or more days. Each event comes with an anonymous random identifier created when you install the app and basic technical details such as the operating system version, device type and app version.

These events never contain the names of tasks you type yourself. They are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat). Subscriptions are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a subscription is valid and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives the purchase record and an anonymous app user identifier. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What Tameby does not do

  • No advertising inside the app and no advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID).
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your schedule.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera or photo library.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. Anonymous analytics events are kept by PostHog for a limited period and cannot be tied back to you; if you want them removed, write to the address below with the date you installed the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat as required to honour your subscription; to cancel a subscription, use the subscription settings of your Apple ID or Google account.

Children

Tameby is for adults keeping a home. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change.
